Acquired-rights synthetic media

The covered files are synthetic visual media reused under rights acquired from a
third party. They are not first-party Libre AI works: Libre AI does not hold
their copyright and grants no licence in them.

Per ADR 0008, the proof of rights and the full provenance record are held
outside this repository, and the delivered files deliberately carry no trace of
their source. The generating model is not recorded. Consequently no outbound
licence can be asserted here, and none is: these files are published as
pedagogical study material only, under the terms of the acquisition, and are
excluded from every blanket licence declared for this repository.

Every media review sheet for these files is currently `status: draft` with
`decision: rework`, so they are excluded from the served corpus by the
fail-closed gate until a human bias review approves them.

Downstream recipients acquire no right to redistribute or reuse these files.
Anyone needing such rights must obtain them from the original rightsholder.
This marker records the absence of a grant; it is not itself a licence.
